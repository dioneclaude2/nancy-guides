Overall

Crystal and Susan’s feedback

01

Sound effects, balanced

We are using them, which is good. Now the levels. Enough to make the video interesting, never enough to sit on top of it — they belong behind the edit, not in front of it.

02

Use more of the footage

There is a lot from 28 to 30 August nobody has touched. Be holistic about it — go through the whole folder before deciding what to cut.

03

Stickers, images, props

Adding clothing or props onto the interviewer worked really well — more of that. Stickers and images make an interview fun rather than flat.

04

Be mindful of colour correction

Some clips come in needing a grade. Notice it before you build the edit around it and raise it — a finished piece cut from ungraded footage is a re-do.

05

Work to a target

Each batch should have a goal, not just a count. Decide what a day of footage is for before you start cutting it.

Five really good pieces beat fifteen rushed ones.
Fonts

Three fonts. One of them does most of the work.

We all stay on the same fonts, so the grid does not read as four different editors.

Default
Helvetica

Text hooks, memes, street interviews, captions — all Helvetica. As close to Instagram’s own font as possible. Clean and uniform beats decorated.

Event highlight titles
Fraunces · PP Editorial

Only on event highlight titles — “A little Nancy moment in NYC” is one. Around 20px. Not on memes, street interviews or text hooks.

The exception
When a look is requested

If Susan asks for a specific font or a current vibe, that wins. Google it, use Google Images to match it, replicate the treatment. Case by case.

Choosing clips

Watch it all before you cut any of it.

01

Go through the whole day

The strongest moments are being missed because the folder was never watched to the end.

02

Do not reuse a clip

If it has already been used, find another one. The same shot with a new caption is not a new post.

03

Five strong beats ten filler

We would rather have fewer edits that were each chosen on purpose.

04

Some clips just need a straight cut

Short and direct works. Not everything needs building.

Music

Where the next hook comes from

How to spot an outlier

An outlier is a post that beat its own account’s normal by a wide margin. Not the biggest account — the biggest gap. That gap is what tells you the idea did the work, which means it can be copied.

1

Something stops you

Loads of comments, or copy that is genuinely good.

2

Tap the account

The number means nothing until you know their normal.

3

Scroll their last 30 days

Just look at the view counts. No maths, no tools.

Not an outlier

Everything they post does this. The account did the work.

Outlier

Obviously higher than the rest. Take the hook shape and fit it to our footage.

If you have to squint to tell, it is a no.
